## Check-in Quiz 1: From Associate to Team Lead (5 questions)

1. Which statement best describes the difference between leading and managing?
   A) Leading is for senior people; managing is for team leads
   B) Leading guides people; managing organizes work  ✔
   C) Leading means giving orders; managing means being friendly
   D) There is no difference
   Rationale: Leading is about people (direction, trust, growth). Managing is about work (planning, tracking, solving). A team lead does both.

2. Priya was your top performer. As a new lead she spends all day clearing tickets herself while her team waits for guidance. Which shift has she missed?
   A) From peer to leader
   B) From doing to enabling  ✔
   C) From team results to my results
   D) From email to chat
   Rationale: Her job now is to enable the team to perform, not to be the fastest doer.

3. Which is NOT a core responsibility of a team lead?
   A) Communicating expectations
   B) Coaching and giving feedback
   C) Personally doing all the difficult tasks  ✔
   D) Escalating issues
   Rationale: Doing everything yourself does not scale and leaves the team without growth.

4. A brand-new associate does not yet know the process. Which style fits best at first?
   A) Delegating
   B) Directive  ✔
   C) Hands-off
   D) Avoiding
   Rationale: New people need clear steps and direction. Move toward coaching and delegating as they grow.

5. Which style suits a skilled, confident associate who works best with freedom?
   A) Delegating  ✔
   B) Directive
   C) Micromanaging
   D) Silence
   Rationale: Skilled, confident people thrive with ownership, which is delegating.

## Check-in Quiz 2: Communication Essentials (5 questions)

1. Which is the best email subject line?
   A) Update
   B) Urgent!!!
   C) Action needed by Fri 5 PM: clear 12 pending refund cases  ✔
   D) Hi
   Rationale: A good subject names the action, the deadline and the topic.

2. Where should the main request appear in an email?
   A) In the last line
   B) In the first lines (BLUF)  ✔
   C) Only in an attachment
   D) Anywhere
   Rationale: Bottom Line Up Front means readers see the ask immediately.

3. You must give sensitive feedback to an associate. Which channel is best?
   A) Team channel post
   B) Email to the whole team
   C) Private call or face-to-face meeting  ✔
   D) A chat emoji
   Rationale: Sensitive topics need a private, live conversation where tone can be heard and seen.

4. Which does NOT belong in a 5-minute huddle?
   A) Wins
   B) Targets
   C) Blockers
   D) Long public criticism of one person  ✔
   Rationale: Huddles are short and positive. Criticism is handled privately.

5. Active listening includes…
   A) Interrupting to fix the problem
   B) Paraphrasing what you heard  ✔
   C) Checking your phone
   D) Planning your reply while they talk
   Rationale: Paraphrasing shows you understood and invites correction.

## Check-in Quiz 3: Open Conversations (5 questions)

1. Which is an open question?
   A) Are you okay?
   B) Did you finish?
   C) What has been the hardest part of your week?  ✔
   D) Is that fine?
   Rationale: Open questions start with what, how or tell me about, and invite more than yes or no.

2. In O.P.E.N., what does “Echo” mean?
   A) Repeat your own point louder
   B) Paraphrase what you heard  ✔
   C) Ask the same question again
   D) End the conversation
   Rationale: Echoing confirms understanding and shows you listened.

3. The associate pauses for a long time before answering. What is best?
   A) Fill the silence with advice
   B) Wait calmly for a few seconds  ✔
   C) Change the topic
   D) End the meeting
   Rationale: Silence gives space for the real answer.

4. What does the N in O.P.E.N. stand for?
   A) Notes
   B) Next steps  ✔
   C) Negotiate
   D) Nothing
   Rationale: Agree specific next steps, owner and date.

5. A good way to begin a one-on-one is to…
   A) Start with performance criticism
   B) Check in on the person’s wellbeing  ✔
   C) Read out the scoreboard
   D) Talk only about your own problems
   Rationale: A wellbeing check-in builds trust and sets a safe tone.

## Check-in Quiz 4: Difficult Conversations (5 questions)

1. SBI stands for…
   A) Situation, Behavior, Impact  ✔
   B) Say, Brief, Inform
   C) Speak, Behave, Improve
   D) Skill, Boundary, Intent
   Rationale: SBI keeps feedback factual and specific.

   A) You are careless
   B) In yesterday’s audit, 6 of 20 cases had wrong amounts  ✔
   C) You never care
   D) You are lazy
   Rationale: Behavior statements describe what happened, not who the person is.

3. An associate becomes defensive during feedback. What is the best first response?
   A) Raise your voice
   B) Acknowledge the feeling and return to the facts  ✔
   C) Drop the issue
   D) Threaten consequences
   Rationale: Acknowledging emotion lowers defenses; facts keep the discussion on track.

4. When should you involve HR or your manager?
   A) For any small mistake
   B) For harassment, threats or safety concerns  ✔
   C) Never
   D) Only after a year
   Rationale: Serious conduct and safety issues need formal handling.

5. What is the best setting for a difficult conversation?
   A) Team channel
   B) Private, planned and live  ✔
   C) Corridor chat
   D) Email only
   Rationale: Privacy and planning protect dignity and make agreement more likely.

## Check-in Quiz 5: Accountability and Ownership (5 questions)

1. Accountability means…
   A) Blaming the right person
   B) Owning the outcome, not just the task  ✔
   C) Doing everything yourself
   D) Avoiding mistakes at any cost
   Rationale: Accountability is owning results and what happens next.

2. Which email habit works best?
   A) Check only when someone calls
   B) Check constantly all day
   C) Check at set times: start, middle and end of shift  ✔
   D) Check once a week
   Rationale: A steady rhythm keeps people from waiting without wrecking your focus.

3. A client asks for data by Friday and you cannot answer today. Best reply?
   A) Say nothing until Friday
   B) “Received. I will send it by Thursday 3 PM.”  ✔
   C) “Will check.”
   D) Forward it to the team without comment
   Rationale: Acknowledge and commit to a time so the client does not need to chase.

4. You find a mistake you made that affects the client. What is best?
   A) Hope nobody notices
   B) Wait until the next review
   C) Tell them early, own it, fix it and say how you will prevent it  ✔
   D) Blame the process
   Rationale: Own, Fix, Prevent builds trust.

5. Which phrase shows ownership?
   A) “That’s not my job.”
   B) “I will find out who can help and confirm back by 3 PM.”  ✔
   C) “Nobody told me.”
   D) “Not my team.”
   Rationale: Ownership means taking the next step yourself.

## Check-in Quiz 6: Deliverables, SLAs and SOW (5 questions)

1. Which is a SMART deliverable?
   A) Improve quality
   B) Do better on calls
   C) Each associate keeps audit accuracy at 98% or above this month  ✔
   D) Work harder
   Rationale: It is specific, measurable and time-bound.

2. “First reply to every client email within 1 hour” is which SLA type?
   A) Resolution time
   B) Response time  ✔
   C) Availability
   D) Productivity
   Rationale: Response time measures how fast the first reply happens.

3. “98% accuracy on audited cases” is which SLA type?
   A) Quality / accuracy  ✔
   B) Turnaround time
   C) CSAT
   D) Availability
   Rationale: It measures correctness of work.

4. The client asks for an extra weekly report that is not in the SOW. What should you do?
   A) Just do it to keep them happy
   B) Refuse and ignore the request
   C) Raise a change request through the agreed route  ✔
   D) Ask an associate to do it quietly
   Rationale: New work outside scope goes through change control so effort, time and cost are agreed.

5. CTIS stands for…
   A) Context, Target, Impact, Support  ✔
   B) Check, Tell, Inform, Send
   C) Clarify, Track, Improve, Solve
   D) Call, Text, Inform, Save
   Rationale: CTIS is a simple structure for explaining deliverables.

## Check-in Quiz 7: Workload and Goal Management (5 questions)

1. Coaching and process improvement are important but not urgent. What should you do with them?
   A) Drop them
   B) Schedule time for them  ✔
   C) Delegate to a client
   D) Do them only in a crisis
   Rationale: Important but not urgent work is where growth happens. Put it on the calendar.

2. 6 associates each have 6 productive hours. Demand is 45 hours. What is the gap?
   A) 6 hours
   B) 9 hours  ✔
   C) 12 hours
   D) 36 hours
   Rationale: Capacity is 6 × 6 = 36 hours. Demand 45 minus 36 gives a 9-hour gap.

3. Which is a sign of overload?
   A) Rising errors and late replies  ✔
   B) Everyone finishing early
   C) Quiet huddles
   D) More coffee breaks
   Rationale: Errors and delays often signal overload before people say anything.

4. What is the right order for a goal cascade?
   A) Individual, team, client
   B) Client SLA, team goal, individual goal  ✔
   C) Team, client, individual
   D) Any order
   Rationale: Individual goals should connect to the team goal, which supports the client SLA.

5. A goal turns Amber. What is best?
   A) Wait until month end
   B) Find the blockers early and adjust the plan  ✔
   C) Hide it
   D) Blame the team
   Rationale: Amber is your early warning to act.
